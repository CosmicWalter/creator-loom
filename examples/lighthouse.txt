Chapter 1: The blue signal

On a quiet coast, a lighthouse keeper named Rowan saw a blue light between two distant rocks. The light appeared three times, paused, and appeared once more. Rowan wrote the pattern on a scrap of paper instead of guessing what it meant.

Chapter 2: The small mirror

At dawn, Rowan carried a small mirror to the tower window. The sea was calm enough to reveal a boat beyond the rocks. Rowan repeated the pattern using sunlight. A person on the boat lifted a white cloth in reply.

Chapter 3: The shared notebook

The boat reached the harbor before noon. Its navigator had found an old signal book with one page missing. Rowan shared the recorded pattern, and together they added the missing explanation to a new notebook. Neither claimed to have understood the message alone.
